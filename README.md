# 4D Reservoir Monitoring & AI-Assisted Interpretation Platform

An interactive decision-support platform for analysing time-lapse seismic data, investigating reservoir changes, and identifying potential opportunities for improved hydrocarbon recovery.

## Overview

The **4D Reservoir Monitoring & AI-Assisted Interpretation Platform** supports reservoir surveillance through the integrated analysis of baseline and monitor seismic surveys. By examining changes in seismic responses over time, the platform helps geoscientists and reservoir engineers investigate potential fluid movement, identify anomalous response zones, and assess areas that may warrant further investigation.

The platform combines **seismic attribute analysis, machine learning, well-data integration, and interactive 3D visualisation** within a unified workflow. Its objective is to transform complex 4D seismic observations into interpretable geological evidence and actionable investigation priorities.

## Key Capabilities

- **4D Seismic Analysis** — Compare baseline and monitor surveys to characterise time-lapse seismic changes.
- **Seismic Attribute Analysis** — Calculate and examine amplitude differences, normalised RMS differences (NRMS), and other relevant seismic attributes.
- **AI-Assisted Anomaly Detection** — Detect, classify, and cluster anomalous seismic response patterns for further investigation.
- **Well Data Integration** — Incorporate available well logs, checkshots, deviation surveys, and geological markers to provide subsurface context.
- **Reservoir Change Assessment** — Investigate potential fluid-movement patterns and identify areas of limited apparent seismic change.
- **Explainable Opportunity Ranking** — Prioritise anomalous zones using transparent, evidence-based criteria rather than treating detection scores as confirmed hydrocarbon probabilities.
- **Interactive 3D Visualisation** — Explore seismic volumes, attribute maps, detected zones, well locations, and time-lapse changes through an integrated dashboard.
- **AI-Assisted Interpretation** — Use language-model assistance to summarise analytical evidence and support geological interpretation.

## Scientific Approach

The platform distinguishes between **observed seismic changes, computationally detected anomalies, and geological interpretations**.

Since time-lapse seismic responses can be influenced by acquisition differences, processing effects, noise, and geological conditions, detected anomalies are treated as investigation targets rather than definitive evidence of fluid movement or remaining hydrocarbons.

## Project Objectives

- Develop an explainable AI-assisted 4D seismic interpretation workflow.
- Improve reservoir surveillance through integrated seismic and well-data analysis.
- Investigate potential fluid movement and identify areas that may warrant further assessment for bypassed hydrocarbons.
- Provide transparent, evidence-based investigation priorities.
- Support informed geoscientific and operational decision-making.
