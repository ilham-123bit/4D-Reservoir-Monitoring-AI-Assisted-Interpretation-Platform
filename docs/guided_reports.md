# Guided interpretation reports

The guided report brings a selected seismic finding, its calculated attributes, detection evidence, available well context and limitations into a single review sequence. The same finding identity and grid indices are retained across linked sections and report summaries.

## Report contents

- Analysis scope and data readiness.
- Baseline and Monitor measurements with signed difference and valid support.
- Statistical and eligible ML screening evidence.
- Spatial component extent and data-quality coverage.
- Available well records and the status of any proposed association.
- Alternative explanations, uncertainty and a next verification step.

An investigation-priority rank is local to its documented analysis scope; it is not a geological probability. When coordinates, registration or well depth/time references are unverified, the report states that the association is unavailable.

## Presentation

The finding-directed storyboard automatically centers the sample plane and intersecting inline/crossline views on a valid member of the selected component. Baseline and Monitor are the measured vintages. Any blended frame is display interpolation only and does not represent acquired reservoir evolution. The offline HTML export is generated from the same completed finding record and interpretation ledger.

The workflow supports review and prioritization of further investigation. A candidate does not establish fluid movement, remaining hydrocarbons or a production opportunity.
